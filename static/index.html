Remove the background from any image, free and private

AI removes the background from your photo automatically and gives you a clean transparent PNG or WebP, for product photos, profile pictures, logos, stickers and portraits. Unlike most online background removers, nothing is uploaded: the model runs inside your browser, so your images stay on your device. No sign-up, no watermark, no limits.

How to remove a background from a photo

  1. Add your images

    Drag and drop, click to choose files, or paste with Ctrl+V / ⌘V.

  2. Let the AI work

    The background is removed automatically. The first image takes longer while the model downloads.

  3. Check the result

    Preview on a grid, white or black, and press and hold to compare with the original.

  4. Download

    Save each cut-out, or get them all at once as a ZIP.

Frequently asked questions

Is this background remover free?

Yes. It is completely free, with no sign-up, no watermark and no limit on the number of images.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. The AI model runs inside your browser, on your GPU or CPU. Your photos never leave your device.

What image formats are supported?

You can drop JPG, PNG and WebP images. Results download as transparent PNG or WebP.

Does it reduce the resolution of my photo?

No. The cut-out is applied at full size, up to 4096 pixels on the longest side. You can optionally choose a smaller maximum size.

Why is the first image slow?

The first time, your browser downloads the AI model (90 to 180 MB). It is then cached, so later images take about a second on most GPUs.

Can I remove backgrounds from many images at once?

Yes. Drop as many images as you like; they are processed one after another and you can download them all as a ZIP file.
